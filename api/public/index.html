Automation & control

Manage WAPDA safely. Cloud automation keeps working even while the app is closed.

Auto-shift to WAPDAnighttime only (6 PM\u20136 AM) \u00b7 voltage-triggered
Enable auto-shift
Only arms between 6 PM and 6 AM. Closes the WAPDA relay once battery voltage drops to the trigger below, then rechecks every minute for real grid power before starting the clock \u2014 a closed relay proves nothing if WAPDA itself is down. The charge ends early if PV recovers past the stop threshold, or if the duration runs out.

Relay protection: a cycle never starts if it would have to stop straight away, the relay stays closed for the minimum ON time once switched, and no new cycle can begin until the cooldown has passed. If the breaker is opened by anything else \u2014 the Tuya app, a schedule on the device, or mains cycling it \u2014 the cycle ends instead of switching it back on.
